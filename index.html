😊 About Me

I am Tongbao Chen （陈通宝）, a Lecturer and master's supervisor at Guangdong Polytechnic Normal University.

My research centers on multimodal intelligence and trustworthy AI. My interests include multimodal representation learning, cross-modal retrieval, video understanding, affective computing, AI security, federated learning, and privacy protection.

In multimedia understanding, I investigate temporal sentence grounding, video corpus moment retrieval, and multimodal emotion recognition. My work draws on graph representation learning, contrastive learning, curriculum learning, and cross-modal semantic modeling. My broader research interests also include the integrity verification of large language models and privacy-aware collaborative learning.

I received my master's degree from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2020 and my Ph.D. from Macau University of Science and Technology in 2024. My publications appear in journals including IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems for Video Technology, IEEE Transactions on Multimedia, Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence, Neurocomputing, and Computer Networks.

Looking for research opportunities? I welcome motivated master's students and undergraduates interested in multimodal learning, trustworthy AI, and related topics. Please contact me at tongbaochen@gpnu.edu.cn.

🔬 Research Interests

Multimodal Representation Learning

Semantic alignment and representation learning across text, audio, and visual modalities.

Cross-Modal Retrieval & Video Understanding

Text-video retrieval, temporal sentence grounding, and video corpus moment retrieval.

Affective Computing

Multimodal emotion recognition with complete and missing modalities.

AI Security

Trustworthy AI, large language model fingerprinting, and model integrity verification.

Federated Learning

Collaborative learning and client selection strategies for distributed machine learning.

Privacy Protection

Privacy-aware learning and secure use of data and AI models.
